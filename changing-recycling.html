Our vision

How we will change lithium recycling in Australia

A large focus of Sustainable Lithium Cells Australia is reducing the number of consumer lithium batteries — particularly embedded ones — from entering landfill.

The problem

Suppliers are de-incentivised to take responsibility

The general recycling industry and government consensus is that product suppliers should take stewardship for the safe disposal of products they bring to market. But suppliers are de-incentivised to do this due to prohibitive additional costs — leaving disposal fees in the hands of unaware consumers, often with recycling information buried in small print.

Our company has been honing efficient, cost-positive dismantling and resource recovery of lithium batteries since 2021. The technology required is not advanced — but it does require experience in methodologies, custom tooling, and an understanding of various product designs. These skills allow us to be the only providers of completely free — or most discounted — recycling to many Australian businesses that have taken it upon themselves to reconsolidate their end-of-life products.

Our approach

Incentivise businesses, empower communities

Using a straightforward, openly replicable approach, we incentivise businesses to take responsibility for encouraging consumers to return end-of-life products to consolidation points — most obviously, the place where they were purchased.

R&D feedback

Through systematic dismantling, we provide batch fault data back to businesses — valuable product intelligence at no extra cost.

Social enterprise

Through our partner Substation33, recovered resources power social enterprise initiatives including solar storage for remote communities.

B-Cycle integration

We aim to bring this model into the B-Cycle program to demonstrate how embedded product disposal costs can be passed to consumers for a negligible amount.

Reducing fire risk

This model will significantly reduce the burden on reactive measures for lithium disposal dangers — including detection systems and fire response in general waste streams.

The goal

Towards 95%+ recycling rates

Our current business model is a gateway and role model for how lithium battery recycling does not need to be costly to individuals or businesses. It is a stepping stone to the 95%+ recycling rates seen in lead-acid battery recycling — where people are actually paid for their scrap batteries.

Scaling to a noticeably impactful level requires co-operation from businesses willing to advertise their ability to reclaim end-of-life products. We need industry support and awareness. Long-term supply contracts offer significant financial and sustainability benefits — contact us to discuss.
