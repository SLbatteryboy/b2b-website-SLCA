How it works

From collection to second life

Contact us or fill out the form

Email, call, or use our Google Form to describe your battery — size, type, condition, and location. Include photos if you have them.

We assess and quote

We'll confirm whether your battery is suitable for collection, purchase, or disposal and provide pricing. Most QLD businesses will pay less than with any other provider.

Collection or drop-off arranged

We organise pick-up or you can drop off at our Brisbane facility. Conditions apply for free pick-ups.
