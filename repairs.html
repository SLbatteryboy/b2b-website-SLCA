Getting a repair

How the repair process works

If you're looking to get a battery repaired, include photos, a description of the fault, and your location with your enquiry. It is difficult to quote exact repair costs upfront, so we offer a $100 diagnostic and quote fee for batteries brought in for assessment. For many simple faults, this may cover the full cost of the work.

For complex repairs, anticipate potential rebuild quotes from $100–$1,000 depending on the size and complexity of your battery. For electric motorbikes, larger EV packs, or solar batteries where cell replacements are necessary, the cost may exceed this range.
