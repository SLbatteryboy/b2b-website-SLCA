Lithium battery safety resources

Working with lithium batteries requires solid foundational knowledge. At minimum, newcomers should understand thermal runaway — the primary risk associated with all lithium-ion batteries.

Online training resources

We've compiled the following courses and resources to help you handle and work with lithium batteries safely.

Understanding the Risks of Lithium-Ion Battery Systems — UNSW Sydney Free self-paced course (~4 hrs) covering battery system risks and safety practices.
Anatomy of Lithium-Ion Batteries — GSES Two-part webinar explaining chemistry, construction, and hazards of lithium batteries.
Solar Battery System Fundamentals — GSES 4-hour online course on battery storage with PV systems, maintenance, and safety.
Understanding Lithium-Ion Battery Fire Risk — Storemasta Academy Video course on fire risks, handling, and storage of Li-ion batteries.
Li-ion Battery Safety Training — Hilti Australia 45-minute training for power-tool battery users; safety features and risk management.
Lithium-Ion Battery Safety Awareness for Superyachts — Superyacht Crew Academy Safety procedures and hazard awareness tailored to marine environments.
